Core Capabilities

Everything a Barangay
Needs to Stay Safe

From data entry to AI-powered risk forecasting — all in one locally-hosted platform.

Blotter Management
Log, search, update, and archive blotter entries with full docket tracking — criminal and civil cases in one place.
Incident Reporting
Capture detailed incident records with location, time, category, priority level, and responding officer assignments.
Settlement Monitoring
Track compliance to settlement awards. Monitor agreed terms, execution dates, and compliance status in real time.
Heat Map Analysis
Visualize incident density across barangay zones with color-coded risk grids and clickable zone breakdowns.
ML Predictions
Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting models forecast incident likelihood by zone, time window, and category, trained and evaluated live on your barangay's own records.
Automated Reports
Generate PDF and Excel reports on demand or on schedule — incident summaries, risk assessments, patrol plans and more.
The Process

From Data Entry to
Actionable Insight

BlotterCast turns your barangay's records into a living intelligence system — automatically.

01
Data Ingestion
Log or Import Records
Encode blotter entries and incident reports directly, or batch-import legacy CSV and Excel files with automatic data cleaning.
02
Feature Extraction
Spatial & Temporal Analysis
The system automatically extracts zone coordinates, time-of-day patterns, seasonal trends, and repeat-offense signals.
03
Machine Learning
Predictive Model Training
Multiple ML algorithms compete on your data. The best-performing model is activated and auto-retrained as new records arrive.
04
Decision Support
Act on Forecasts
Officials receive risk alerts, patrol recommendations, and exportable reports — enabling proactive community safety decisions.
